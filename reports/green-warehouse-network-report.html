Green Warehouse Network Report

Confirmed Objective: CO2 Reduction

Scenario Requests and Results

Scenario 1: Default Scenario

The first scenario was executed with the objective of reducing CO2 emissions using five warehouses. The results are as follows:

  • Annual Cost: €1,252,442
  • Total CO₂ Emissions: 379.229 t
  • Opened Warehouses:
    • W01 - Hamburg logistics hub
    • W04 - Hannover logistics hub
    • W09 - Mannheim logistics hub
    • W10 - Nürnberg logistics hub
    • W11 - Stuttgart logistics hub
  • Closed Warehouses: None

Scenario 2: Closure of Hamburg Site

The second scenario involved closing the Hamburg site (W01) and opening Bremen logistics hub (W03). The results were:

  • Annual Cost: €1,220,676
  • Total CO₂ Emissions: 397.438 t
  • Opened Warehouses:
    • W03 - Bremen logistics hub
    • W04 - Hannover logistics hub
    • W09 - Mannheim logistics hub
    • W10 - Nürnberg logistics hub
    • W11 - Stuttgart logistics hub
  • Closed Warehouses:
    • W01 - Hamburg logistics hub

Feasibility and Warnings

Both scenarios were executed successfully without any critical failures. However, the second scenario resulted in a closure of the Hamburg site, which affected customer assignments. The solver encountered a time limit issue in the third scenario, leading to no assignment changes.

Conclusion

The analysis indicates that while the closure of the Hamburg site resulted in a slight reduction in costs, it also led to an increase in CO₂ emissions. The overall impact of the scenarios demonstrates the complexity of balancing cost and emissions in warehouse management. Further adjustments may be necessary to optimize both objectives effectively.
