# Future GTM sprints

These sprints follow the current Germany-wide warehouse-network planning MVP.

## Sprint 1 — Design-partner pilot

- **Goal:** Prove that logistics planners can use scenario-based planning to make a warehouse-network decision faster and with enough confidence to bring the result into an operating review.
- **Target user/buyer:** Network planning managers and supply-chain transformation leads at German 3PLs, retailers, and manufacturers; the economic buyer is the head of supply chain or logistics.
- **Channel or motion:** Founder-led outbound to a small set of German logistics organizations, supported by a live workshop using each prospect's anonymized network assumptions.
- **Key deliverable:** A guided pilot package with onboarding checklist, customer-data import template, baseline-versus-scenario review, and a repeatable decision-review deck.
- **Success metric:** Recruit 5 design partners; at least 4 complete two scenario reviews and 3 confirm that the output is decision-ready for an internal planning meeting.

## Sprint 2 — Planning-to-action integrations

- **Goal:** Turn a completed plan into a repeatable operating workflow so teams receive changes and exceptions where they already work.
- **Target user/buyer:** Operations planners and supply-chain IT owners in pilot accounts; the buyer is the logistics operations director or digitalization lead.
- **Channel or motion:** Expansion within design-partner accounts, with implementation-led selling and a short integration demo for new prospects.
- **Key deliverable:** An n8n integration pack that can schedule scenario runs, export results to Sheets or a BI destination, and send Slack/email notifications for infeasibility, warehouse closures, or material cost/CO₂ changes.
- **Success metric:** Launch 3 production-like account workflows; at least 60% of completed scenarios in those accounts produce a viewed or acknowledged downstream notification within one business day.

## Sprint 3 — Repeatable commercial package

- **Goal:** Package the product around measurable resilience, cost, and CO₂ outcomes and create a repeatable path from first conversation to paid pilot.
- **Target user/buyer:** Mid-market 3PL and retail supply-chain leaders who own network design or decarbonization budgets; secondary audience is consulting and logistics-technology partners.
- **Channel or motion:** Publish one quantified case study and ROI calculator, then use targeted content, partner referrals, and conference/webinar demos to generate qualified discovery calls.
- **Key deliverable:** A paid-pilot offer with standard implementation scope, ROI worksheet, security/data FAQ, case study, and partner demo environment.
- **Success metric:** Generate 10 qualified opportunities, convert at least 3 to paid pilots, and achieve a 25% or higher discovery-to-pilot conversion rate.
